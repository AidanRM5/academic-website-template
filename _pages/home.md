### About me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Philosophical Moral Psychology Lab at the Uehiro Oxford Institute. My research intersects social psychology, cognitive science, moral philosophy and ecology, examining how cognitive structure influences our moral and political judgements, behaviours, beliefs and belief systems. I completed a PhD at the University of Melbourne, MA at Macquarie University, and a BSc/BA from the University of New South Wales.
